• 6-3 Deal: Players inspect 6 cards before calling Trump. If all pass, Dealer is Uckered (forced to name Trump). Then the final 3 cards are dealt.
• Strict Trick Play: You must follow suit. If Trump is led (or you trump in while void), you must climb (beat the highest Trump in the trick if able). If void in the led suit, you must play Trump if you hold any!
• Melds & Bella: 3 in a row (`6-7-8` to `Q-K-A`) = +20 pts; 4 in a row = +50 pts. Holding K + Q of Trump = +20 pts ("Bella!").
• Getting Baited: Total base points = 162 (152 card pts + 10 for Last Trick). If the team that called Trump fails to win strictly more than half the total points in play, they are Baited (score 0, opponents take all points!).
